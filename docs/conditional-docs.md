# Conditional docs

One line per feature doc in `app_docs/`, saying when to read it; read the lines whose situation matches your task.

- Read `app_docs/landing-page.md` when changing the page layout, copy, height variants, the reel slider or reel cards, adding a reel, touching the no-scroll specs, or changing link previews (Open Graph, Twitter image, favicon).
- Read `app_docs/checkout.md` when changing the checkout form, the server action, the price, Stripe Checkout parameters or tax, the thank-you dialog, or the E2E Stripe mock, or the macOS 14 WebKit server (`.adw/worktree-env`).
- Read `app_docs/analytics.md` when changing PostHog start-up, the `?internal` tag, adding, renaming or removing an event, touching UTM handling, or adding or changing a short link (`apps/web/src/content/links.ts`, the redirects in `apps/web/next.config.ts`).
- Read `app_docs/confirmation-email.md` when changing the Stripe webhook, the confirmation email or owner notice copy, Resend, the email variables, or the E2E Resend mock.
- Read `app_docs/legal-pages.md` when changing the terms or privacy pages, the provider facts or placeholders in `apps/web/src/content/legal.ts`, the complaints book link, or anything the legal texts describe (price, reel length, footage window, data processors, analytics cookies).
- Read `app_docs/studio.md` when changing anything under `apps/studio`, the `/studio` forwarding (`apps/web/src/lib/studio-rewrites.ts`, `assetPrefix`), the magic-link login, the `reels`, `jobs` or `steps` tables, their RLS or migrations, the upload to R2, `claim_job` and the lease, `apps/worker`, `packages/storage`, the storage and AI test doubles, or the studio's DB and E2E tests.
- Read `app_docs/measurement.md` when changing the step runner, step measurements or their columns, the summary or error-tail rules, OpenTelemetry logs and traces in `apps/worker`, or the studio's PostHog start-up, identify or `studio_*` events.
- Read `app_docs/adw-supabase-stack.md` when changing `.adw/supabase-stack`, the Supabase part of `.adw/worktree-env`, the ADW stack's ports or reset rule, `SUPABASE_DB_URL`, or how runs apply migrations.
- `app_docs/pipeline.md`: read when changing the pipeline steps (convert, transcribe, retime, background removal, head tracking, preview copies), fingerprints and the R2 cache, output `kept` or `rebuildable` tags, the retime planner or preset, the head-track format, preview sizes, the transcript format, HyperFrames or ffmpeg arguments, `test:tools` or `fixtures/pipeline/`.
- Read `app_docs/edit-model.md` when changing `packages/edit` (the edit format, preset, builder, HarfBuzz measuring, checks, versions), the `versions` table or `save_version`, `reels.current_version_id`, the worker's `first_version` step, or the browser test layer.
- `app_docs/preview.md`: read when changing the studio live preview, the HyperFrames player wrapper or its version pin, the preview clock or sound scheduling, the edit-assets route, `reels.preview_copies`, or `apps/studio/e2e/preview.spec.ts`.
- `app_docs/decide-step.md`: read when changing the decide step (`apps/worker/src/decide/`), its prompt or `PROMPT_VERSION`, the per-reel answer schema in `packages/edit/src/decide.ts`, the glossary check, the AI runner or its `E2E_MOCK_AI_SCRIPT` double, or the `versions` decide records and `save_version` parameters.
- `app_docs/change-requests.md`: read when changing the change box, the versions list or going back (`apps/studio/src/components/ChangeRequestForm.tsx`, `VersionList.tsx`, `change-actions.ts`); when changing the `change` or `restore` job kinds, their columns or insert policy, or the worker's `job-kinds.ts` and `restore-step.ts`; when changing the one-job-at-a-time rule.
