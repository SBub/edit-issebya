# Conditional docs

One line per feature doc in `app_docs/`, saying when to read it; read the lines whose situation matches your task.

- Read `app_docs/landing-page.md` when changing the page layout, copy, height variants, the reel slider or reel cards, adding a reel, touching the no-scroll specs, or changing link previews (Open Graph, Twitter image, favicon).
- Read `app_docs/checkout.md` when changing the checkout form, the server action, the price, Stripe Checkout parameters or tax, the thank-you dialog, or the E2E Stripe mock, or the macOS 14 WebKit server (`.adw/worktree-env`).
- Read `app_docs/analytics.md` when changing PostHog start-up, the `?internal` tag, adding, renaming or removing an event, touching UTM handling, or adding or changing a short link (`apps/web/src/content/links.ts`, the redirects in `apps/web/next.config.ts`).
- Read `app_docs/confirmation-email.md` when changing the Stripe webhook, the confirmation email or owner notice copy, Resend, the email variables, or the E2E Resend mock.
- Read `app_docs/legal-pages.md` when changing the terms or privacy pages, the provider facts or placeholders in `apps/web/src/content/legal.ts`, the complaints book link, or anything the legal texts describe (price, reel length, footage window, data processors, analytics cookies).
- Read `app_docs/studio.md` when changing anything under `apps/studio`, the `/studio` forwarding (`apps/web/src/lib/studio-rewrites.ts`, `assetPrefix`), the magic-link login, the `reels`, `jobs` or `steps` tables, their RLS or migrations, the upload to R2, `claim_job` and the lease, `apps/worker`, `packages/storage`, the storage and AI test doubles, `delete_reel`, the reels list's bin (`DeleteReelButton`), `storage_deletions` and the worker's deletion pass, or the studio's DB and E2E tests.
- Read `app_docs/measurement.md` when changing the step runner, step measurements or their columns, the summary or error-tail rules, OpenTelemetry logs and traces in `apps/worker` (the `reel.*` span attributes, `reel-size.ts`), the step-time-against-reel-length insight, or the studio's PostHog start-up, identify or `studio_*` events.
- Read `app_docs/adw-supabase-stack.md` when changing `.adw/supabase-stack`, the Supabase part of `.adw/worktree-env`, the ADW stack's ports or reset rule, `SUPABASE_DB_URL`, or how runs apply migrations.
- `app_docs/pipeline.md`: read when changing the on-demand background map (`backgrounds.json`) or the pipeline steps (convert, transcribe, retime, head tracking (with its sampled person mask), preview copies), fingerprints and the R2 cache, output `kept` or `rebuildable` tags, the retime planner or preset, the head-track format, preview sizes, the transcript format, HyperFrames or ffmpeg arguments, the on-demand cut-out (the `cutout` step's masks, the MODNet mask tool (`onnxruntime-node`, `modnet-entry.ts`, `matte.ts`), the model pin and `models:install`, `WORKER_MODELS_DIR`, the doctor's mask tool line), `test:tools` or `fixtures/pipeline/`.
- Read `app_docs/edit-model.md` when changing `packages/edit` (the edit format, the `behind` track (VISUAL corner cards and big words behind the speaker), masks in `inputs`, preset (its `pace`, the intensity level), builder and its cut-out composite, HarfBuzz measuring, checks including the `safe-box` and `text-overlap` checks, versions, transitions tied to a phrase (`transitions.ts`, the `numbered` kind, its text levels; the LIST answer `lists`, its items numbered per list; what a new answer may hold, LIST lists of items and no title), the written hook (the `hook` overlay kind, `pace.hook`, its timing, must-place priority and render), the layout engine's input in `engine-input.ts`, the layout engine in `engine.ts` and `engine-space.ts` (timing, scene, free space, placement by priority, the engine record) and the robust face `facesOver`), the `versions` table or `save_version`, `reels.current_version_id`, the worker's `first_version` step, the platform safe zone (`PLATFORM_SAFE`, `PLATFORM_BUTTONS`), or the browser test layer; when changing readability (contrast, the background map and sampler, the low-contrast note).
- `app_docs/preview.md`: read when changing the studio live preview, the HyperFrames player wrapper or its version pin, the preview clock or sound scheduling, the edit-assets route, `reels.preview_copies`, the platform zones toggle, or `apps/studio/e2e/preview.spec.ts`.
- `app_docs/decide-step.md`: read when changing the decide step (`apps/worker/src/decide/`), its prompts (fetched from PostHog by name and label: `reel-hook`, `reel-components`; `apps/worker/src/prompts/`, `POSTHOG_PERSONAL_API_KEY`, `POSTHOG_PROJECT_ID`, `POSTHOG_PROMPT_LABEL`, the last good copy served for 60 minutes when PostHog does not answer, the `E2E_MOCK_PROMPTS` placeholders), the hook call (`hook.ts`: the written hook, its own AI call before the components, its schema and retry, its glossary check), the per-reel answer schema in `packages/edit/src/decide.ts` (meaning only: word ids and a VISUAL's short description; the ACCENT, BIGWORD, VISUAL and LIST answer lists; the hook's length caps), the components' answer (no density target, the overlap limits the schema does not count), the glossary check, the rhythm of extras and the pace (the engine's, not the prompt's), the behind lengths and the absence of a per-reel count, why layout never refuses an answer, and the stored checklist (`versions.checklist`, read back only), and how the engine record is saved and logged (`versions.engine_record`), the AI runner (`apps/worker/src/ai.ts`: OpenRouter through the Vercel AI SDK, `AI_MODEL` and `AI_MODELS`, `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, the price table, the JSON mode fallback, the error mapping) or its `E2E_MOCK_AI_SCRIPT` double, the version's AI record (`<version id>.ai.json`, `ai-record.ts`) or the decision trace (`trace.ts`, `decision-trace.ts`), or the `versions` decide records and `save_version` parameters.
- `app_docs/change-requests.md`: read when changing the versions list or going back (`restore` jobs, `VersionList.tsx`, `change-actions.ts`), Start over (`start_over` jobs, `StartOverButton.tsx`, `start-over.ts`), their columns or insert policy, or the worker's `job-kinds.ts` and `restore-step.ts`; when changing when going back or Start over is blocked or queued; or when touching the removed change box (no UI sends a `change` job, and the worker fails one).
- `app_docs/export.md`: read when changing the Export button, the `render` job, the `exports` table, download links, or the preview-matches-export guardrail and its SSIM bound (`apps/studio/e2e-tools/export-matches-preview.spec.ts`).
- Read `app_docs/upload.md` when changing the footage upload (`UploadForm`, the uploader, the upload routes under `apps/studio/src/app/api/uploads/s3/multipart/`), the multipart methods of `packages/storage`, `finish_upload` or `fail_upload`, `reels.source_upload_id`, the resume record, or the storage mock route's part `PUT`.
- Read `app_docs/storage.md` when changing `stored_files`, `worker_status`, `record_stored_file`, the slimming or export expiry, scratch cleanup, the scratch folder's creation at worker start-up, the doctor's R2 write probe, the disk guard, `delete_reel`, `storage_deletions` and its functions, the deletion pass (`storage-deletions.ts`), `recordingStorage`, `E2E_MOCK_FREE_DISK_GB` or the studio's waiting reason.
- Read `app_docs/worker-container.md` when changing the worker's Docker image or compose files (`apps/worker/Dockerfile`, `compose.yaml`, `compose.prod.yaml`, `.dockerignore`), the MODNet model baked into the image or its pin in the Dockerfile, the scratch volume, the container's environment overrides, the doctor run inside the container, or running the worker in a container on the Mac.
- Read `app_docs/status.md` when changing the reel page's progress overlay (`ProgressOverlay.tsx`, `progress-overlay.ts`), the step words (`step-words.ts`), the overlay's failure actions or `studio_failure_action`, the `New version N` notice or the automatic version switch of the preview, step progress (`steps.progress_percent`, the parsers or the throttle), the time-left estimate or `reels.footage_seconds`, `worker_status.heartbeat_at` and the `Worker offline since` line, keep-awake (`caffeinate`), or `E2E_MOCK_RENDER_SECONDS`.
