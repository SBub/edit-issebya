# Conditional docs

One line per feature doc in `app_docs/`, saying when to read it; read the lines whose situation matches your task.

- Read `app_docs/landing-page.md` when changing the page layout, copy, height variants, the reel slider or reel cards, adding a reel, touching the no-scroll specs, or changing link previews (Open Graph, Twitter image, favicon).
- Read `app_docs/checkout.md` when changing the checkout form, the server action, the price, Stripe Checkout parameters or tax, the thank-you dialog, or the E2E Stripe mock, or the macOS 14 WebKit server (`.adw/worktree-env`).
- Read `app_docs/analytics.md` when changing PostHog start-up, the `?internal` tag, adding, renaming or removing an event, touching UTM handling, or adding or changing a short link (`apps/web/src/content/links.ts`, the redirects in `apps/web/next.config.ts`).
- Read `app_docs/confirmation-email.md` when changing the Stripe webhook, the confirmation email or owner notice copy, Resend, the email variables, or the E2E Resend mock.
- Read `app_docs/legal-pages.md` when changing the terms or privacy pages, the provider facts or placeholders in `apps/web/src/content/legal.ts`, the complaints book link, or anything the legal texts describe (price, reel length, footage window, data processors, analytics cookies).
- Read `app_docs/studio.md` when changing anything under `apps/studio`, the `/studio` forwarding (`apps/web/src/lib/studio-rewrites.ts`, `assetPrefix`), the magic-link login, the `reels`, `jobs` or `steps` tables, their RLS or migrations, the upload to R2, `claim_job` and the lease, `apps/worker`, `packages/storage`, the storage and AI test doubles, or the studio's DB and E2E tests.
- Read `app_docs/measurement.md` when changing the step runner, step measurements or their columns, the summary or error-tail rules, OpenTelemetry logs and traces in `apps/worker` (the `reel.*` span attributes, `reel-size.ts`), the step-time-against-reel-length insight, or the studio's PostHog start-up, identify or `studio_*` events.
- Read `app_docs/adw-supabase-stack.md` when changing `.adw/supabase-stack`, the Supabase part of `.adw/worktree-env`, the ADW stack's ports or reset rule, `SUPABASE_DB_URL`, or how runs apply migrations.
- `app_docs/pipeline.md`: read when changing the pipeline steps (convert, transcribe, retime, head tracking (with its sampled person mask), preview copies), fingerprints and the R2 cache, output `kept` or `rebuildable` tags, the retime planner or preset, the head-track format, preview sizes, the transcript format, HyperFrames or ffmpeg arguments, the on-demand cut-out (the `cutout` step's masks, the `vseg` mask tool, `build:vseg`, the doctor's mask tool line), `test:tools` or `fixtures/pipeline/`.
- Read `app_docs/edit-model.md` when changing `packages/edit` (the edit format, the `behind` track (photos and one big word behind the speaker), masks and photos in `inputs`, preset, builder and its cut-out composite, HarfBuzz measuring, checks including the `frame` check, versions), the `versions` table or `save_version`, `reels.current_version_id`, the worker's `first_version` step, the platform safe zone (`PLATFORM_SAFE`), or the browser test layer.
- `app_docs/preview.md`: read when changing the studio live preview, the HyperFrames player wrapper or its version pin, the preview clock or sound scheduling, the edit-assets route, `reels.preview_copies`, the platform zones toggle, or `apps/studio/e2e/preview.spec.ts`.
- `app_docs/decide-step.md`: read when changing the decide step (`apps/worker/src/decide/`), its prompt or `PROMPT_VERSION`, the per-reel answer schema in `packages/edit/src/decide.ts`, the glossary check, the behind limits (2 photos, 1 big word) or the reel's `behind_rules` forbid flags, the `behind-face` and `behind-caption` checks, the AI runner or its `E2E_MOCK_AI_SCRIPT` double, or the `versions` decide records and `save_version` parameters.
- `app_docs/change-requests.md`: read when changing the change box, the versions list or going back (`apps/studio/src/components/ChangeRequestForm.tsx`, `VersionList.tsx`, `change-actions.ts`); when changing the `change` or `restore` job kinds, their columns or insert policy, or the worker's `job-kinds.ts` and `restore-step.ts`; when changing when the change box is blocked or queued.
- `app_docs/export.md`: read when changing the Export button, the `render` job, the `exports` table, download links, or the preview-matches-export guardrail and its SSIM bound (`apps/studio/e2e-tools/export-matches-preview.spec.ts`).
- Read `app_docs/upload.md` when changing the footage upload (`UploadForm`, the uploader, the upload routes under `apps/studio/src/app/api/uploads/s3/multipart/`), the multipart methods of `packages/storage`, `finish_upload` or `fail_upload`, `reels.source_upload_id`, the resume record, or the storage mock route's part `PUT`.
- Read `app_docs/storage.md` when changing `stored_files`, `worker_status`, `record_stored_file`, the slimming or export expiry, scratch cleanup, the scratch folder's creation at worker start-up, the doctor's R2 write probe, the disk guard, `recordingStorage`, `E2E_MOCK_FREE_DISK_GB` or the studio's waiting reason.
- Read `app_docs/status.md` when changing the reel page's progress overlay (`ProgressOverlay.tsx`, `progress-overlay.ts`), the step words (`step-words.ts`), the overlay's failure actions or `studio_failure_action`, the `New version N` notice or the automatic version switch of the preview, step progress (`steps.progress_percent`, the parsers or the throttle), the time-left estimate or `reels.footage_seconds`, the ready notification and `Ready: <reel>` title, `worker_status.heartbeat_at` and the `Worker offline since` line, keep-awake (`caffeinate`), or `E2E_MOCK_RENDER_SECONDS`.
- Read `app_docs/failures.md` when changing job failures, transient errors or the one automatic retry, the overlay's Try again (retry) or `retry_of_job_id` and its policy, `jobs.failure_kind` and `failure_rules`, `waiting_ai`, `waiting_reason` or `not_before`, the per-reel order in `claim_job`, the footage check (`check_footage`) or its refusal messages, or the step double (`E2E_MOCK_STEPS`, `E2E_MOCK_STEP_FAULT`).
- Read `app_docs/dashboard.md` when changing the PostHog dashboard script or its insights (`apps/studio/scripts/posthog-dashboard/`), a synced table's columns or `updated_at` trigger, the table prefix, or a metric definition (including `step-time-vs-length`).
- Read `app_docs/error-tracking.md` when changing `onRequestError`, `reportFailure` or a handled server failure in the studio or the landing page, the studio's `captureException` or the upload failure capture, the worker's error tracker or crash handlers, `worker: job failed`, `studio: realtime channel dropped`, the local development error log (`dev-error-log.ts`, `yarn dev:studio errors`), or what an exception may carry.
- `app_docs/studio-reel-layout.md`: read when changing the reel page layout (`ReelScreen.tsx`, the phone's Edit, Versions and Library tabs, the no-scroll rule, the overlay's position over the preview), the read-only tracks timeline or Undo and Redo; when changing the reels thumbnail grid, `UploadDialog` or the thumbnail and frames sprite made by the `preview_copies` step; when touching the dark theme tokens or `apps/studio/e2e/reel-layout.spec.ts`.
- `app_docs/photo-library.md`: read when changing the photo library (the `photos` table, `describe_photo` and `photo_images` steps, the upload, `PhotoLibrary` and its Library tab); when giving Claude Code any tool or file (`readFile`); when a job, step or stored file may belong to a photo instead of a reel (`photo_id`).
